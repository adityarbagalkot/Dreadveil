# Character Roster

The original ten archetypes are retained and ten new character concepts are proposed below. New additions' HP and cooldown values are playtest baselines, not final balance guarantees. Roster entries are design specifications, not evidence of implementation.

|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---|

The source roster currently uses archetype titles. Add approved personal names without discarding these identities.

Visual direction for additions: upright humanoid animal hybrids with two arms, two legs, usable weapons, and anatomy visibly shaped by their animal traits. Keep the dark, grotesque, combat-ready fantasy style and avoid reusing animal species from the original ten for the new hybrids.

Detailed effect timing and edge cases are in [Ability Contracts](abilities.md).
