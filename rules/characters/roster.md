## Character identities and combat roles

### 1. Darius Forgeward — Forge Tank
- **Role fantasy:** immovable front-line bulwark who punishes enemies for attacking.
- **Visual silhouette:** broad, low center of gravity; rhino horn and plated tortoise shell; badger claws; layered forge-metal armor.
- **Weapon:** oversized anvil maul.
- **Ability — Forgeguard:** gain +10 Guard this turn. If the enemy's current attack damages Darius, deal 3 scorch damage to the enemy once for that action.
- **Design read:** defense and retaliation; visually heavy rather than agile.

### 2. Corven Vex — Raven
- **Role fantasy:** evasive occultist who makes the enemy's current move fail.
- **Visual silhouette:** narrow raven head and beak, black feather mantle, octopus-like secondary tendrils used for reach and grip.
- **Weapon:** paired ink-black hooked blades.
- **Ability — Void Feint:** negate the enemy's current action, including attached components unless an encounter explicitly marks one unavoidable.
- **Design read:** denial and trickery, not sustained tanking.

### 3. Maelis Rotvein — Plaguewright
- **Role fantasy:** attrition specialist who wins by leaving a sickness behind.
- **Visual silhouette:** hunched hyena frame, axolotl gills, leech-like tendrils and wet alchemical apparatus.
- **Weapon:** blight staff with sealed infection vessels.
- **Ability — Lingering Infection:** poison the enemy for 3 damage at the end of each of the next four turns. The first tick is on a later turn, not immediately on application.
- **Design read:** delayed damage; weaker immediate burst in exchange for pressure over time.

### 4. Serath Oathscar — Oathbreaker
- **Role fantasy:** duelist whose vows turn enemy aggression into punishment.
- **Visual silhouette:** antlered stag skull profile, vulture feather mantle, segmented scorpion tail integrated into the armor silhouette.
- **Weapon:** broken oath-glaive.
- **Ability — Vengeful Oath:** if the enemy attacks this turn, retaliate for its full declared attack damage, even if Guard prevents the incoming damage.
- **Design read:** strongest when reading and surviving an attack; less valuable against defensive intents.

### 5. Ilyra Veilseer — Oracle
- **Role fantasy:** foresight tactician who improves dice choices with limited knowledge.
- **Visual silhouette:** owl face and facial disk, moth-wing mantle, a contained anglerfish-like lure-lantern; avoid making the lure resemble a second head.
- **Weapon:** lantern sceptre.
- **Ability — Threadseer:** gain two extra single-die rerolls this turn and reveal only the enemy's next intent.
- **Design read:** information and control of variance, not unrestricted future vision.

### 6. Garruk Bloodhowl — Bloodhowl
- **Role fantasy:** self-wounding berserker who converts danger into explosive offense.
- **Visual silhouette:** boar tusks and shoulders, wolf-like muzzle, bat ears and folded membrane wings.
- **Weapon:** bloodied great-axe.
- **Ability — Blood Frenzy:** pay 5 HP. Strike dice are doubled, or tripled if HP is below one-third maximum at activation. Snapshot the multiplier at activation.
- **Design read:** high-risk offense; HP cost and threshold must be visible before confirmation.

### 7. Vaelis Thornwake — Thornwarden
- **Role fantasy:** living briar sentinel who arrests an enemy's momentum.
- **Visual silhouette:** mantis forearm blades, deer antlers and legs, overlapping pangolin scales with thorn growth.
- **Weapon:** thorn glaive.
- **Ability — Rootbind:** prevent the enemy's current action, including its secondary effects unless an encounter explicitly marks a component unavoidable.
- **Design read:** action denial; high strategic value, so its cooldown and boss interactions need scrutiny.

### 8. Tovin Gearwright — Gear-Saint
- **Role fantasy:** ritual mechanic who alters the value of a carefully chosen die.
- **Visual silhouette:** frog-like face and powerful thighs, armadillo plates, beetle elytra shaped into a mechanical mantle; brass mechanisms should feel grafted, not clean steampunk.
- **Weapon:** chronometer hammer.
- **Ability — Clockwork Overwind (recommended replacement):** after rolling, choose one die; add +3 to that die's contribution for this turn after its natural face rule is applied. This does not change the six-die pool.
- **Design read:** precision and manipulation. This replaces the conflicting extra-die concept to preserve the locked six-dice rule.

### 9. Elsin Mourningcall — Dirgesinger
- **Role fantasy:** mournful performer who turns an enemy's violence into recovery.
- **Visual silhouette:** fox muzzle and ears, peacock eye-feather mantle, cicada wing casings and ribbed resonant chest.
- **Weapon:** resonance war-lyre, played or struck in combat.
- **Ability — Requiem Reversal:** if the enemy attacks this turn, convert the damage components of that action into healing, capped at maximum HP. Separately described status effects are not automatically removed.
- **Design read:** conditional recovery; weak against non-attack intents.

### 10. Nymor Hollowstar — Hollow Star
- **Role fantasy:** void-touched gambler who sacrifices vitality for perfect rolls.
- **Visual silhouette:** hare ears and elongated limbs, abyssal eel jawline and translucent fin structures, anglerfish lure embedded in the brow or crown.
- **Weapon:** void scythe.
- **Ability — Perfected Ruin:** pay 6 HP and set each of the six dice to its own maximum legal face value. Never set every die to the literal value 6.
- **Design read:** overwhelming certainty at a steep cost; test carefully because six maximum dice can dominate both Strike and Guard.

### 11. Borik Thornwall — The Ironhide
- **Role fantasy:** thorn-armored counterattacker.
- **Visual silhouette:** wolverine claws and shoulders, porcupine quills along the back and forearms, musk-ox mane and heavy brow.
- **Weapon:** barbed war-pick.
- **Ability — Quillguard:** gain +6 Guard. If the current enemy attack damages Borik, return 3 damage once during that enemy action.
- **Design read:** durable retaliation; less action-denial than Raven or Thornwarden.

### 12. Kaelen Redtalon — The Red Talon
- **Role fantasy:** execution specialist who finishes weakened foes.
- **Visual silhouette:** secretary-bird legs and crest, shoebill beak and heavy skull profile, caracal ear tufts and compact feline musculature.
- **Weapon:** executioner's glaive.
- **Ability — Execution Mark:** gain +4 Strike damage. After normal Strike resolves, if the enemy has 15 HP or less remaining, deal an additional 6 damage. Do not apply the bonus if the enemy is already defeated.
- **Design read:** strong finishing pressure; weakens against high-HP targets early in a fight.

### 13. Sszareth Mirefang — The Miremaw
- **Role fantasy:** armored predator that opens a breach in enemy defenses.
- **Visual silhouette:** gharial snout and teeth, monitor-lizard body and tail, horned-lizard spines and frill.
- **Weapon:** serrated cleaver.
- **Ability — Armor Rend:** deal 5 direct damage that bypasses Guard, then reduce enemy Guard by 2 for its next defensive intent.
- **Design read:** setup for a later Strike; direct damage and defense reduction must remain separate effects.

### 14. Ormund Deepdelver — The Burrow King
- **Role fantasy:** subterranean fortress that rewards a committed defensive stance.
- **Visual silhouette:** aardvark snout and claws, echidna spines, tapir-like nose and dense digging forelimbs.
- **Weapon:** earthbreaker hammer.
- **Ability — Earthbound Fortress:** gain +10 Guard and a further +3 if at least three dice are assigned to Guard.
- **Design read:** defense-heavy; the assignment threshold creates a meaningful cost in Strike output.

### 15. Thalric Stormwake — The Drowned King
- **Role fantasy:** tide-born hunter who weakens a coming attack while pressing forward.
- **Visual silhouette:** albatross wings and long reach, frigatebird throat pouch and hooked beak, cormorant neck and slick plumage.
- **Weapon:** tide-splitter spear.
- **Ability — Undertow:** gain +4 Strike damage and reduce the enemy's next attack by 4, to a minimum of zero.
- **Design read:** balanced offense and mitigation; the attack reduction applies only once.

### 16. Veyra Cindercrawl — The Red Furnace
- **Role fantasy:** caustic ambusher who punishes either enemy defense or predictable aggression.
- **Visual silhouette:** velvet-worm antennae and soft segmented hide, giant-centipede body plates and many subtle limb cues, trap-jaw ant mandibles.
- **Weapon:** caustic twin-blades.
- **Ability — Caustic Sheath:** reduce enemy Guard by 4 for its next defensive intent. If its next intent is not defensive, gain +4 Strike damage this turn instead.
- **Design read:** conditional pressure. The engine may inspect the next intent to resolve the branch, but the UI must not reveal it.

### 17. Rovan Blackstride — The Black Strider
- **Role fantasy:** relentless flightless-bird charger who converts momentum into a guarded recovery.
- **Visual silhouette:** cassowary casque and vivid neck accents, emu-like shaggy plumage, rhea proportions and long runner legs.
- **Weapon:** rupture lance.
- **Ability — Breakneck Charge:** gain +8 Strike damage. If the enemy survives the player's Strike resolution, gain +4 Guard for the enemy action.
- **Design read:** rewards offense without making survival automatic.

### 18. Nyra Gloamveil — The Gloamstalker
- **Role fantasy:** dusk predator who slips an attack while exploiting an aggressive stance.
- **Visual silhouette:** genet spots and long body, civet facial markings, binturong shag and prehensile-tail silhouette.
- **Weapon:** shadow hook-blades.
- **Ability — Shadowstep:** negate damage from the enemy's current attack. If at least three dice are assigned to Strike, gain +4 Strike damage. Separately described status effects still apply.
- **Design read:** conditional evasion with an offensive assignment incentive.

### 19. Eryndor Palevein — The Pale Deep
- **Role fantasy:** ancient amphibious survivor that restores itself when near collapse.
- **Visual silhouette:** giant-salamander folds and broad head, caecilian ringed body details, olm-like pale skin and vestigial eyes.
- **Weapon:** ossuary staff.
- **Ability — Abyssal Regrowth:** heal 7 HP, or 10 HP if below half maximum HP at activation. Healing is capped at maximum HP.
- **Design read:** sustain rather than prevention; healing cannot rescue a character after defeat has already triggered.

### 20. Sylvius Moonrift — The Nightglider
- **Role fantasy:** gliding skirmisher who corrects two bad rolls without spending the standard reroll.
- **Visual silhouette:** flying-squirrel membrane between limbs, colugo facial proportions and broad gliding membrane, sugar-glider eye markings and tail.
- **Weapon:** crescent daggers.
- **Ability — Slipstream:** reroll up to two selected dice without spending the standard reroll. Each selected die may be rerolled once during the activation, and new results must be kept.
- **Design read:** consistency and flexible assignment; does not guarantee high results.

## Identity consistency
- Display the personal name as the primary label and archetype/title as the epithet.
- Preserve each hybrid's distinct silhouette; do not make every character a human body with a cosmetic animal head.
- Default anatomy is two arms, two legs, and a readable weapon, unless approved concept art explicitly establishes otherwise.
- New hybrids (11–20) must not reuse species from the original ten.
- Visual effects must support role readability without obscuring silhouette or weapon.
- Refer to [Ability Contracts](abilities.md) and [Character Visual Standards](visual-standards.md) for authoritative details.
