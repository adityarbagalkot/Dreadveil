# Character Profiles

This document supplies character-card copy and concise narrative foundations for the twenty-character roster. These are proposed creative details for review, not previously established canon. They should support the documented mechanics rather than introduce undocumented combat rules.

## 1. Darius Forgeward — Forge Tank
**Epithet:** The Last Anvil  
**Personality:** Patient, blunt, protective; speaks only after everyone else has finished arguing.  
**Lore:** Darius once guarded the furnace-gates of a city that fed its dead into the forge to keep its walls warm. When the gates failed, he held the breach alone until the city went dark. He now carries the anvil that sealed the furnace, convinced that endurance is the last honest virtue.  
**Motivation:** Prevent another settlement from being sacrificed for survival.  
**Flaw:** Mistakes suffering endured for suffering solved.  
**Strength:** Reliable defense and punishment for enemies that attack.  
**Weakness:** Less efficient when the enemy refuses to attack or can bypass Guard.  
**Card copy:** “Become the wall. Make every blow cost the enemy.”

## 2. Corven Vex — Raven
**Epithet:** The Unwritten Omen  
**Personality:** Sardonic, observant, evasive; never answers the question that was asked.  
**Lore:** Corven stole a page from a death-priest's ledger and found his own death recorded on it. The page was blank everywhere else. Since then, he has hunted the hand that writes the ledger, using shadow and ink-black tendrils to interrupt the moments that should have been inevitable.  
**Motivation:** Find the author of his fate and erase his entry.  
**Flaw:** Trusts disruption more than commitment.  
**Strength:** Cancels a dangerous current enemy action.  
**Weakness:** Action denial is cooldown-limited and does not improve every ordinary turn.  
**Card copy:** “The enemy makes its move. Corven edits it out.”

## 3. Maelis Rotvein — Plaguewright
**Epithet:** The Gentle Contagion  
**Personality:** Soft-spoken, clinical, unsettlingly compassionate toward the dying.  
**Lore:** Maelis served in a hospice where no patient recovered, yet none died. She learned that a living body can become a prison when sickness is managed instead of cured. Now she carries carefully sealed cultures, seeking the original blight that taught the flesh to fear its own renewal.  
**Motivation:** Discover whether the plague can be reversed without transferring it to another host.  
**Flaw:** Treats people as evidence when under pressure.  
**Strength:** Sustained damage over several turns.  
**Weakness:** Poison needs time; a quick fight reduces its value.  
**Card copy:** “The wound closes. The sickness stays.”

## 4. Serath Oathscar — Oathbreaker
**Epithet:** The Debt Collector  
**Personality:** Formal, severe, scrupulously fair by a code no one else agreed to.  
**Lore:** Serath swore an oath to protect a royal procession and obeyed every clause while the procession was slaughtered by a loophole in the wording. He broke the oath publicly, carved its seal into his armor, and began collecting the debts left by promises that were technically kept.  
**Motivation:** Destroy contracts that use precise language to excuse cruelty.  
**Flaw:** Cannot distinguish justice from repayment.  
**Strength:** Retaliation against attack intents, even when Guard absorbs the hit.  
**Weakness:** Gains little from the ability when the enemy defends or uses non-attack actions.  
**Card copy:** “Every strike creates a debt. Serath always collects.”

## 5. Ilyra Veilseer — Oracle
**Epithet:** The Lantern at the End  
**Personality:** Calm, curious, privately afraid of being certain.  
**Lore:** Ilyra's moth wings grew after she followed a lantern through a ruined observatory and saw one second of tomorrow. She has spent years learning that foresight is not command: knowing the next disaster only changes what one is willing to sacrifice before it arrives.  
**Motivation:** Learn how much of the future can be changed without creating something worse.  
**Flaw:** Delays choices while searching for a cleaner outcome.  
**Strength:** Extra rerolls and limited next-intent foresight.  
**Weakness:** Foresight is narrow and does not reveal the whole encounter.  
**Card copy:** “See one step beyond the veil. Spend that knowledge wisely.”

## 6. Garruk Bloodhowl — Bloodhowl
**Epithet:** The Red Hunger  
**Personality:** Fiercely loyal, impatient, ashamed of how easily anger makes decisions for him.  
**Lore:** Garruk was bred as a war-beast and taught that pain was proof of obedience. When he broke his chain, he kept the iron collar and turned its links into the haft of his axe. He now chooses when to bleed, though the old instinct still whispers that the fastest victory is worth any cost.  
**Motivation:** Ensure no one is shaped into a weapon without consent.  
**Flaw:** Treats self-destruction as control.  
**Strength:** Exceptional burst damage when the multiplier is favorable.  
**Weakness:** Pays HP and risks leaving too little margin for enemy damage.  
**Card copy:** “Spend blood now. End the fight before it spends you.”

## 7. Vaelis Thornwake — Thornwarden
**Epithet:** The Briar Sentence  
**Personality:** Quiet, territorial, protective of boundaries and old places.  
**Lore:** Vaelis guarded a grove where the buried dead spoke through roots. When soldiers came to harvest the trees, the grove rose around her and fused bark, scale, and blade into one body. She left the grove only when the roots began repeating the names of people not yet dead.  
**Motivation:** Find who is teaching the buried dead to predict the living.  
**Flaw:** Confuses protection with preventing all change.  
**Strength:** Stops a dangerous enemy action outright.  
**Weakness:** Powerful action denial is limited by cooldown and timing.  
**Card copy:** “The briars close. Even violence must wait.”

## 8. Tovin Gearwright — Gear-Saint
**Epithet:** The Second Hand  
**Personality:** Precise, restless, delighted by small corrections; distrusts miracles without mechanisms.  
**Lore:** Tovin built a clock that could measure the instant before a person made a choice. It stopped whenever he tried to use it on himself. He now travels with its broken heart mounted in a hammer, adjusting small probabilities rather than pretending he can command time.  
**Motivation:** Discover why the clock refuses to measure his own decisions.  
**Flaw:** Believes every failure is a calibration problem.  
**Strength:** Improves one chosen die without changing the fixed dice pool.  
**Weakness:** The bonus is limited and requires choosing the right die.  
**Card copy:** “A single precise correction can change the whole equation.”

## 9. Elsin Mourningcall — Dirgesinger
**Epithet:** The Last Refrain  
**Personality:** Tender, theatrical, attentive to grief; fears silence more than death.  
**Lore:** Elsin learned to play a tune that was sung at funerals before the dead arrived. When the melody reached its final note, the mourners found their wounds closing while the singer grew colder. Elsin now carries the instrument from village to battlefield, trying to learn where comfort ends and theft begins.  
**Motivation:** Find a way to soothe suffering without taking it into herself.  
**Flaw:** Accepts burdens without asking whether they should be carried.  
**Strength:** Can turn an enemy attack's damage into healing.  
**Weakness:** Its value depends on an attack intent and can be wasted at full HP.  
**Card copy:** “Let the enemy's violence become your next breath.”

## 10. Nymor Hollowstar — Hollow Star
**Epithet:** The Empty Constellation  
**Personality:** Gentle, distant, frighteningly decisive once a choice is made.  
**Lore:** Nymor was found in a crater beneath a starless sky, alive beside a map of constellations that had never existed. The lure in his brow glows when a possibility collapses. He believes the void is not hungry; it simply demands that every certainty be paid for.  
**Motivation:** Learn what was removed from the sky to make room for him.  
**Flaw:** Treats certainty as worth any personal cost.  
**Strength:** Turns every die into its own maximum legal result.  
**Weakness:** Pays HP and can become dangerously exposed after activation.  
**Card copy:** “For one perfect moment, uncertainty dies. So does a little of you.”

## 11. Borik Thornwall — The Ironhide
**Epithet:** The Quill Bastion  
**Personality:** Gruff, practical, protective of strangers who cannot protect themselves.  
**Lore:** Borik grew up among caravan guards who used their bodies as shields for travelers crossing a region of falling black glass. When the company was scattered, he forged the broken guard-plates into a coat of barbs. He now stands between danger and anyone too tired to run.  
**Motivation:** Rebuild a road where no traveler has to pay for protection with their life.  
**Flaw:** Assumes help must hurt to be genuine.  
**Strength:** Adds Guard and returns a small amount of damage when attacked.  
**Weakness:** Less effective against non-attack intents and overwhelming hits.  
**Card copy:** “Come closer. The armor has teeth.”

## 12. Kaelen Redtalon — The Red Talon
**Epithet:** The Final Measure  
**Personality:** Disciplined, direct, impatient with grand speeches before a necessary act.  
**Lore:** Kaelen once served as a royal executioner, trained to recognize the exact instant a sentence became irreversible. After discovering that the court sold pardons to the powerful, Kaelen abandoned the dais and began hunting the people who believed consequences were only for the weak.  
**Motivation:** Make sure power does not decide who is allowed a second chance.  
**Flaw:** Can mistake decisiveness for moral clarity.  
**Strength:** Adds Strike damage and a conditional finishing blow.  
**Weakness:** The finishing effect is less useful while the enemy has substantial HP remaining.  
**Card copy:** “Mark the failing. Deliver the final measure.”

## 13. Sszareth Mirefang — The Miremaw
**Epithet:** The Broken Scale  
**Personality:** Suspicious, patient, bluntly honest about weakness.  
**Lore:** Sszareth survived a marsh where armor grew over every living thing until movement became impossible. He learned to cut the seams rather than fight the shell. The cleaver at his side is made from a scale that refused to harden, a reminder that every defense has a place where it must bend.  
**Motivation:** Find the source of the marsh's living armor.  
**Flaw:** Sees every relationship as a structure to be breached.  
**Strength:** Direct damage followed by a reduction to enemy Guard.  
**Weakness:** The defense reduction matters only if a later defensive intent occurs.  
**Card copy:** “Do not break the wall. Find the seam.”

## 14. Ormund Deepdelver — The Burrow King
**Epithet:** The Root Below  
**Personality:** Stubborn, methodical, unexpectedly fond of small comforts.  
**Lore:** Ormund's people built halls beneath a mountain that slowly sank into a sleeping giant. When the ceiling began to fall, he held the oldest tunnel long enough for the last families to escape. He still carries the hammer used to shore up the passage and believes a good defense is something built before anyone notices the danger.  
**Motivation:** Find a permanent refuge for those displaced by the waking earth.  
**Flaw:** Would rather hold a failing position than admit it must be abandoned.  
**Strength:** Large Guard bonus rewarded by assigning several dice to defense.  
**Weakness:** A defensive assignment sacrifices potential Strike.  
**Card copy:** “Set your feet. Let the world break around you.”

## 15. Thalric Stormwake — The Drowned King
**Epithet:** The Salt Crown  
**Personality:** Regal without warmth, observant, protective of the powerless at sea.  
**Lore:** Thalric was crowned by shipwreck survivors who needed a captain after the sea swallowed their fleet. He refused a throne on land and fashioned a crown from the rings of drowned bells. He believes every wave is a debt returning to shore, and every voyage must account for what it leaves behind.  
**Motivation:** Discover why the sea has begun returning ships that sank centuries ago.  
**Flaw:** Treats every obligation as his alone to fulfill.  
**Strength:** Combines a Strike bonus with mitigation against a later attack.  
**Weakness:** Attack reduction is single-use and may be wasted on a weak hit.  
**Card copy:** “The tide takes its due. Thalric decides the amount.”

## 16. Veyra Cindercrawl — The Red Furnace
**Epithet:** The Caustic Saint  
**Personality:** Curious, irreverent, unnervingly cheerful around dangerous substances.  
**Lore:** Veyra was raised in a furnace-colony where the workers' tools slowly dissolved into their hands. She learned to treat corrosion as a language: metal reveals where it is weakest by the way it fails. Her blades carry a controlled version of the colony's acid, and she searches for a cure that does not require another sacrifice.  
**Motivation:** End the furnace-colony's cycle of consuming its workers.  
**Flaw:** Experiments when caution would be wiser.  
**Strength:** Punishes a future defense or gains a Strike bonus if the condition does not apply.  
**Weakness:** The effect's value depends on the next intent.  
**Card copy:** “Every defense has a melting point.”

## 17. Rovan Blackstride — The Black Strider
**Epithet:** The Unbroken Sprint  
**Personality:** Competitive, loud, generous after victory, unbearable before it.  
**Lore:** Rovan carried messages across a battlefield where roads vanished under ash. He learned to run before the ground finished collapsing and to keep moving after every other runner stopped. The lance he carries was once a mile-marker, cut down when the old roads were declared lost.  
**Motivation:** Reopen the routes between isolated settlements.  
**Flaw:** Measures worth by forward motion and struggles to retreat.  
**Strength:** High Strike bonus with conditional Guard if the enemy survives.  
**Weakness:** The defensive benefit disappears when the enemy falls to the Strike.  
**Card copy:** “Hit hard. Keep moving. Never give the ground back.”

## 18. Nyra Gloamveil — The Gloamstalker
**Epithet:** The Borrowed Shadow  
**Personality:** Playful, watchful, evasive about her own needs.  
**Lore:** Nyra stole shadows from people who had already lost their names. She discovered that a shadow can remember the last danger its owner escaped, but never why they ran. She now uses her stolen cloak to slip past attacks while trying to return each shadow to the person it belongs to.  
**Motivation:** Restore the stolen identities before the shadows forget their owners.  
**Flaw:** Avoids direct conflict even when honesty would resolve it.  
**Strength:** Negates current attack damage and rewards an aggressive dice assignment.  
**Weakness:** Does not automatically cancel attached status effects.  
**Card copy:** “Let the blade pass through the place you were.”

## 19. Eryndor Palevein — The Pale Deep
**Epithet:** The Sleeper Below  
**Personality:** Slow-spoken, compassionate, alien in its sense of time.  
**Lore:** Eryndor woke in a flooded crypt after a century of dreaming through stone. The dead around him had not decayed; they had simply become quiet. He learned to knit flesh by listening to the body's oldest rhythm and now searches for the source of the silence beneath the crypt.  
**Motivation:** Discover what has been calling the dead away from their rest.  
**Flaw:** Waits too long for wounds and grudges to heal on their own.  
**Strength:** Reliable self-healing, stronger below half HP.  
**Weakness:** Healing is capped and does not prevent the next attack.  
**Card copy:** “The deep remembers how to mend what the world forgets.”

## 20. Sylvius Moonrift — The Nightglider
**Epithet:** The Moon's Errand  
**Personality:** Wry, nimble, curious, uncomfortable staying anywhere long enough to be mourned.  
**Lore:** Sylvius glided between towers of a city that disappeared one district at a time each new moon. The membranes along his arms carry pale scars shaped like maps of streets that no longer exist. He travels ahead of the vanishing line, searching for a way to keep one place from being erased.  
**Motivation:** Find the source of the moonlit disappearances and save the remaining districts.  
**Flaw:** Leaves before others can ask him to stay.  
**Strength:** Rerolls two selected dice without spending the standard reroll.  
**Weakness:** Improves options but does not guarantee high rolls.  
**Card copy:** “The first fate is a guess. Take another two steps.”

## Roster-wide voice and lore rules
- Keep prose gothic and specific, but avoid making every character grim in exactly the same way.
- Each character needs a distinct verb and motive: hold, edit, infect, collect, foresee, spend, bind, calibrate, convert, perfect, protect, finish, breach, endure, redirect, corrode, charge, evade, regrow, or reroll.
- Lore should explain the character's visual features and combat style without requiring a long exposition in the UI.
- Keep card copy to one short line. Longer lore belongs in an expanded character detail view.
- These profiles do not introduce additional mechanics. Any new gameplay effect must be added to the ability contract and tested separately.
