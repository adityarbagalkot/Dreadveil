## Approved visual direction

- Organic tortoise shell grown into the back and shoulders, not simply a set of armor plates.
- Natural badger fur, heavy build, and digging claws integrated into the body.
- Rhinoceros head and horn establish the dominant head silhouette.
- Reduced, mismatched scavenged armor; keep natural anatomy visible.
- Worn anvil maul, cold ruined setting, muted earthy colors and restrained burgundy accents.
- Hand-painted, textured fantasy art in the Forbidden Manuscript direction; avoid overly busy graphic effects.
- Roster portrait: close-up face, portrait orientation, muted painterly treatment, restrained worn bronze frame.
